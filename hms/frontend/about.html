About us

One record system, built around the patient

MediCore replaces the paper folders and disconnected spreadsheets that slow hospital front desks down, giving every role — admin, doctor, billing staff, and patient — exactly the view they need.

Our Mission

Give small and mid-sized hospitals the same operational clarity as large enterprise systems, without the enterprise price tag.

Who We Serve

Admins running daily operations, doctors managing case loads, billing teams closing invoices, and patients booking care.

Security First

Role-based access, encrypted passwords, and JWT-secured sessions keep every record visible only to the right people.

How it works

Four roles, one shared source of truth

Admin

Oversees patients, doctors, appointments, billing, and revenue reports from a single dashboard.

Doctor

Sees assigned patients and appointments, and updates visit status as care progresses.

Billing Staff

Creates and updates invoices, tracking paid vs. unpaid balances across every patient.

Patient

Books appointments, views assigned doctor and bills, and tracks visit history.
